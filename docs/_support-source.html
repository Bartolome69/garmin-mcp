Support

Email support@daash.run and a person will answer. Say whether you use Claude or ChatGPT, and what you asked when it went wrong.

Quick fixes

  • Garmin won’t take my password. Check it by signing in at connect.garmin.com first. Garmin pauses an account after a few wrong tries, so it is worth checking there before trying again. If Garmin asks you for a code there, you’ll be asked for the same code on Daash’s sign-in page.
  • Sign-in keeps failing. Garmin sometimes turns sign-ins away for a while. Wait an hour and try once more.
  • A workout isn’t on my watch. It goes onto your Garmin calendar first. Sync the watch in the Garmin Connect app and it arrives.
  • I want to disconnect. Open the sign-in page and choose Disconnect. That deletes everything Daash keeps for you. Then remove the connector in Claude or ChatGPT.
